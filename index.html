    Our Story

    Cooked slowly, served generously

    Everything at Olive & Ember comes off one long wood-fired oven and a charcoal grill. Fish arrives whole, vegetables come in from the market two streets over, and nothing is rushed — a whole fish cooks on the bone for twenty minutes before it reaches the table.

    We cook what the season gives us. When tomatoes are at their best, they show up in three dishes. When they are not, they disappear from the menu without ceremony. And when we set the table, it is for sharing — plates go in the middle, everyone reaches across.

    • Seasonal produce The menu is rewritten twice a week around the market.
    • Wood-fired oven One long oven, lit at 10am, hot through dinner service.
    • Family-style sharing Large plates built for the middle of the table.
